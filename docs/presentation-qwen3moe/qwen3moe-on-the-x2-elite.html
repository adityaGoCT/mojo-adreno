All 128 experts

A QNN graph is static: it cannot gather a token’s 8 experts, so it weights all 128. That is 16× the useful work.

The hardware is fast

fp16 weights at about 100 GB/s and 3.4 TFLOP/s of matrix work. With 8 experts read: about 0.8 ms a layer.

Accurate in fp16

Median token within 1.2e-3 of transformers’ layer; QK norms as L2 norms, since QNN’s RmsNorm refuses heads of 128.

Too big as fp16

61 GB against 33.6 GB of RAM. The whole model needs the HTP’s quantized path (w4a16).
