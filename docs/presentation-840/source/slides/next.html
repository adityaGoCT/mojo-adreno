Mojo on the Adreno 840 · 13 · code: adityaGoCT/mojo-adreno · target: adityaGoCT/modular, branch adreno-spirv-target

Three things come next. First, the conversion extension in Mojo itself: today our QCOM kernels are GLSL because LLVM’s SPIR-V backend does not know those instructions; adding them there and to our target would let the same kernels be written in Mojo, and the runners already load them when a driver offers the extension. Second, faster prompts: fp16 products with fp32 sums wherever a driver allows it, and a batched prefill on the matrix units for Qwen3-8B, whose weights are already in the layout our ternary matrix kernel reads. Third, MAX on the phone: its runtime built for Android, so that graphs run on the 840 directly instead of through a hand-written runner per model. To sum up: with one target flag and the driver every Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 phone ships, three language models run on the Adreno 840 and match their references token for token. Thank you.
