Mojo on the Adreno 840 · 8 · bonsai/ · decode: Galaxy S26 Ultra · prefill: Qualcomm’s SM8850 reference phone

The second model is a different kind of test. Bonsai 2 2B is PrismML’s ternary model: every weight is minus one, zero or one, stored in two bits, and it mixes 18 gated DeltaNet layers, which keep a recurrent state instead of a growing cache, with 6 attention layers. Another developer had already ported it to this same GPU in their own fork of Modular’s repository, through a new OpenCL target. Their GPU kernels are plain Mojo, so we took their kernel file as it is and compiled it with our Vulkan target: all 21 kernels compiled and validated without changing a line. What we wrote is the runtime, their forward pass in C. It generates 80 tokens a second, 12.4 milliseconds a token, and for 16 steps every greedy token matches the exporter’s FP32 reference, with all 248 thousand logits compared. Their OpenCL build reports about 75 a second on a regular S26, so the kernels lose nothing through Vulkan. For the prompt we reused their kernels again: a thin wrapper runs a decode kernel on every prompt row in one dispatch. New are three kernels: the ternary projections as matrix products on cooperative matrices, the attention scores too, and the DeltaNet recurrence as one scan with its state in registers. A 412-token prompt takes 0.87 seconds instead of 5.2, six times faster, and the checked tokens still match.
