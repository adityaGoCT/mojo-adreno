Mojo and MAX on a phone GPU

How we ran Mojo kernels and Llama 3.2 1B on a Qualcomm Adreno 710: first by retargeting Mojo’s output, then through a native compiler target, and how far MAX itself is from the phone.

THE QUESTION

Mojo and MAX ship for NVIDIA, AMD and Apple GPUs. Can they reach a phone GPU that only speaks Vulkan and OpenCL?

The device

moto g stylus 5G (2024) with an Adreno 710 GPU, driven over adb from a Mac.

Three drivers

Qualcomm’s stock Vulkan 1.1, Mesa Turnip (Vulkan 1.4, side-loaded) and Qualcomm OpenCL 3.0.

The toolchain

Mojo 1.2 and MAX 26.7 as shipped; later, the Mojo compiler built from source.

Where MAX stopped in 26.7

No SPIR-V target

Mojo’s compiler linked LLVM’s NVPTX, AMDGPU and Apple backends, not SPIR-V.

No Android runtime

MAX shipped for macOS and Linux hosts, with no Vulkan or OpenCL device.

Opaque internals

The 1.2 binary cannot dump Mojo’s MLIR, so a retargeter had to start at LLVM IR.

Vendor-tuned kernels

MAX’s library kernels lean on tensor cores, matrix cores and async copies.

So Llama ran on our own C runtime, and every kernel went through the retargeter.

What we learned

LLVM IR is a workable bridge

A retargeter got Llama running on a GPU Mojo never targeted, exactly.

The losses were lowerings

One warp-sum lowering cost 4.9× on Llama; SPIR-V itself cost little.

Drivers decide

Two Vulkan drivers disagree on speed, and twice on correctness. Check every output.

A native target removes fragility

Same speed, no stand-in target, no text patching. SPIR-V’s limits remain.

MAX’s runtime is the long pole

A new GPU target took about 2,600 lines, a remote driver 1,500 more; MAX on the phone alone is next.

What comes next

The native target

MAX on the phone alone, its runtime built for Android. Then the stock driver, and faster matrix kernels on the 840.

Faster models

Prompts on the matrix units, now 28× (Llama) and 6× (Bonsai) faster, toward the units’ peak: fp16 products with fp32 sums.

Upstream

Send LLVM the structurizer fix, report its strength-reduction crash. Offer Modular the target.

The question is no longer whether Mojo can reach a phone GPU. It is how much of MAX should follow it there.
