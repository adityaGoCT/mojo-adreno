Mojo and MAX on Adreno · 15 · KGEN and POP: Mojo’s internal MLIR dialects

Here is the native pipeline. Everything now happens inside one compiler. Mojo source is parsed and elaborated in Mojo’s own MLIR dialects, KGEN and POP. Because the target is adreno710 from the start, our plugins decide at this stage what a thread index, a barrier or a warp sum becomes: they emit LLVM’s SPIR-V intrinsics directly. Mojo’s normal optimization pipeline runs on the LLVM IR, exactly as it does for NVIDIA and AMD. Then our Vulkan ABI pass reshapes the kernel into a Vulkan entry point, and LLVM 24’s SPIR-V backend, now linked into mojo, writes the binary. Compare with act one: there is no AMD stand-in target any more, no parsing of IR text with regular expressions, no recognizing AMD lane-permute sequences to undo them, and no patching of the finished binary, because LLVM 24 also fixed the barrier bug we patched before. What the target cannot remove are the limits of SPIR-V and the drivers themselves.
