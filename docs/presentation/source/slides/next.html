Mojo and MAX on Adreno · 26 · code: adityaGoCT/mojo-adreno · native target: adityaGoCT/modular, branch adreno-spirv-target

Where next. First, MAX on the phone by itself. The remote device proves the whole path; next, the runtime built for Android and the plugin talking to Vulkan directly, which also removes the round trip per launch. On the compiler side, the native target covers Mesa Turnip only. Qualcomm’s stock driver needs a second flavour: SPIR-V for Vulkan 1.1, and the 32-bit narrowing of index math moved into the backend, where it belongs. The same target could also emit OpenCL C, replacing our second retargeter. And the cooperative-matrix kernels, which now run on an Adreno 840, can go further: staging tiles in shared memory, fusing epilogues so more of MAX’s matmuls qualify, and integer products. For Llama, decode is at 55 percent of the bandwidth floor; int4 weights would cut the bytes read by three quarters, fusing small kernels would cut dispatches, and processing the prompt as matrix products would turn eleven seconds into far less. Upstream, the structurizer fix we patched into LLVM belongs in LLVM itself, the strength-reduction crash deserves a small reproducer, and the warp-sum hook and the target itself are natural contributions to Modular once their compiler accepts outside changes, which they have said will be by the end of 2026. The question we started with, can Mojo reach a phone GPU, is answered. The open question is how much of MAX should follow. Thank you.
